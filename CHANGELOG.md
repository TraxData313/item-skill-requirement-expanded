# Changelog

What changed for players, newest first. Every change lands under **[Unreleased]** the day it is made,
as one short line a player understands (no file names, no internals). At release time the
[Unreleased] lines become the Nexus / Steam change notes, the section is retitled to the new version
and date, and a fresh empty [Unreleased] goes on top.

## [Unreleased]

- Lower tiers cost less: every penalty of a Tier 2 item is now x30%, Tier 3 x50%, Tier 4 x75%. A Tier 3 sword at One Handed 30 loses about 33% damage instead of 67%; 2 kg Tier 2 leather costs about -10% foot speed instead of -34%. Tier 5 and 6 are unchanged. (2026-11-16)
- Three new MCM sliders under "Starter gear": Tier 2 / 3 / 4 penalty scale. (2026-11-16)

## [v1.3.0] - 2026-11-14

The last published release. Its notes and older versions: the GitHub releases page and the Nexus / Steam change notes.
